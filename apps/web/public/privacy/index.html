Tripica · Privacy · Terms

Privacy

Kanter Capital LLC operates Tripica. When you search, Tripica sends the requested places, dates and traveler preferences to its travel data providers. The live service caches results and records fare observations. Cloudflare hosts the service. Tripica uses your IP address for search limits and bot checks. Read the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Trips and sharing

A browser identifier associates your saved plans, notes and watches with this browser on the live service. The demo stores plans in your browser. You can delete saved plans and watches in My trips. A shared link contains a copy of the plan and its notes. Anyone who receives it can read that copy, including after you delete the original.

Visits and booking links

Tripica counts booking clicks by category and page type. The hosting service may keep request logs. When enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits and page performance. Travelpayouts provides affiliate technology that can identify referrals. Booking partners process visits and bookings under their own privacy policies. Tripica may receive a commission.
